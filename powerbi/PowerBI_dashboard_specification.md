# Power BI Dashboard Specification

### Page 1 — Executive Overview
Cards: mean WUENIC coverage, countries, reported cases, diseases.
Charts: annual coverage line, disease cases trend, 2023 MCV1 map, slicers for year/country/antigen/disease.

### Page 2 — Coverage & Drop-off
DTP1 vs DTP3 line chart; DTP1→DTP3 drop-off; lowest-MCV1 country bar chart; antigen/country matrix.

### Page 3 — Disease Impact
Coverage vs incidence scatter; disease cases/incidence trends; 2023 high-coverage/high-measles-incidence table.

### Page 4 — Introduction & Schedule
First introduction year by vaccine and WHO region; introduction status trend; schedule rounds; booster-round share.

### Required slicers
Year, country, WHO region, antigen/vaccine, disease, coverage category, target population.

### Data limitations
The supplied files do not contain gender, education, urban/rural classification, population density, or monthly/date-level observations. Those requested questions cannot be validly answered from these files alone. Coverage–incidence correlations are descriptive associations, not causal vaccine-effectiveness estimates.
